Our mission

AI is becoming part of almost everything we do online. Many apps quietly collect what we write and who we talk to. We believe people deserve a place to connect and share ideas without being watched.

Bees work together in a hive but every bee keeps her own honey. BEESocial is a lightweight prototype that shows what a privacy focused social platform can feel like. It has a real app experience, but it keeps your data on your own device.

Our values

  • Privacy first. We collect as little data as possible. No email, no phone number, and no tracking.
  • Security. Passwords are salted and hashed. The login form limits repeated wrong tries.
  • Freedom of expression. You can buzz with your name or without it.
  • Control. You can edit your profile or delete everything at any time.

How it was built

BEESocial is a multi page static website made with only HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. It has no backend, no database, and no API. All data is saved in the browser using web storage.

This project was made as a midterm activity for a web development course. It is a prototype and not a replacement for a real, audited platform.

What is new in version 2.0

  • Replies. Answer any buzz, with your name or anonymously.
  • Night mode. Switch to a dark hive from the menu. The choice is remembered.
  • Sorting. Show the newest buzzes or the ones with the most honey.
  • Data download. Save a copy of your own data from the settings page.
  • Show password. Check what you typed before you log in.
  • A fresh start. Version 2.0 keeps its own storage, so older prototype data never mixes in.

BEESocial version 2.0 is a student project. No trackers, no cookies, and no servers. Made with honey and code.
