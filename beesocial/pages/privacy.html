Privacy and Security

A plain explanation of what BEESocial does with your data.

What we store

Everything is saved in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

Data Where it is kept Notes
Username, display name, bio, avatar color Local storage You can edit or delete these anytime.
Password Local storage Saved only as a salted SHA-256 hash.
Buzzes, replies, and honey (likes) Local storage Deleted when you delete the item or the account.
Day or night theme choice Local storage Remembers how you like the app to look.
Login session Session storage Cleared when you close the tab.

Every saved item starts with the name beesocial. This keeps BEESocial data separate from older versions and from other apps in your browser.

What we protect against

  • Password theft. We never save the real password. We save a hash made with a random salt.
  • Password guessing. Five wrong tries lock the login form for 30 seconds.
  • Script injection. All user text is cleaned before it is shown on the page.
  • Data ownership. You can download a copy of your data or delete it at any time.
  • Account guessing. Login errors do not say if the username or the password was wrong.
  • Outside tracking. The pages load no outside scripts, fonts, or images.

Honest limits

This is a student prototype. Please keep these limits in mind.

  • Browser storage is not encrypted. Anyone who can open your browser tools on this device can read it.
  • Data does not sync between devices or browsers because there is no server.
  • SHA-256 with a salt is better than plain text but real platforms use slower methods made for passwords.
  • Clearing your browser data will erase your account.

BEESocial version 2.0 is a student project. No trackers, no cookies, and no servers. Made with honey and code.
