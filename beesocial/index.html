Why BEESocial

A hive of your own

There is no server, no database, and no tracking. Everything runs inside your browser.

Your honey stays yours

Passwords are hashed with a random salt. You can delete your account and all your buzzes at any time.

Buzz freely

Post under your name or choose to buzz anonymously. The choice is yours every time.

How it works

  1. Create an account with a username and a strong password.
  2. Write a buzz in the feed and pick if your name is shown.
  3. Send honey to buzzes you like, search the feed, and manage your profile.
  4. Log out or delete everything whenever you want.

BEESocial version 2.0 is a student project. No trackers, no cookies, and no servers. Made with honey and code.
